Courthouse Wedding Photography

Small ceremony.
Still unforgettable.

Getting married at a New Jersey courthouse doesn't mean skipping the photos that matter. Whether it's just the two of you or a small group of witnesses, I cover courthouse weddings and elopements across Northern and Central New Jersey with the same care, honesty, and eye for the quiet, real moments that I bring to every wedding I shoot.

No big production needed just two people, a signature, and someone there to make sure it's remembered well.

Courthouses I Cover

Serving county
clerks across NJ.

Bergen County

Hackensack courthouse ceremonies, plus nearby park and riverside photo spots.

Essex County

Newark courthouse weddings, with downtown Newark and Branch Brook Park as backdrops.

Hudson County

Jersey City courthouse ceremonies, minutes from Liberty State Park and NYC skyline views.

Union County

Elizabeth courthouse coverage, with nearby garden and downtown locations for portraits.

Passaic County

Paterson courthouse ceremonies, with the Great Falls nearby for dramatic portraits.

Not Seeing Your County?

I travel throughout NJ and NY courts reach out and I'll confirm availability.

How It Works

Simple, start
to finish.

We meet outside the courthouse at your scheduled time. I photograph the walk in, the ceremony itself through the window or in the hallway depending on the courthouse's rules, and the moment you walk out married. If you'd like, we can add 20-30 minutes afterward for portraits nearby a park, a quiet street, wherever feels like you.

Most courthouse sessions run 1-2 hours total, start to finish.

Frequently Asked

What couples
usually ask.

Can you take photos inside the courthouse?

It depends on the courthouse some allow photography in the hallway or lobby, others only permit it just outside. I'll confirm the specific rules for your courthouse ahead of time.

How far in advance should we book?

Courthouse ceremonies often get scheduled quickly. I recommend reaching out as soon as you have a date, even if it's just a few weeks out.

Do you offer packages for just the ceremony?

Yes courthouse coverage is priced separately from full wedding packages, with options to add portrait time before or after.

About Catalina

Photography
as a form of
deep listening.

I'm Catalina Perico photographer, storyteller, and founder of Catalina Perico Photography. I capture meaningful moments while helping couples feel comfortable, present, and truly themselves.

I am bilingual in English and Spanish, which means I can connect with you and your family in the language that feels most like home, making everyone feel included from the very first conversation. I take special care to help every woman understand how light enhances her features, how certain angles can be flattering and freeing, and how intentional posing can completely shift the way you feel in front of a camera. I want you to walk away feeling empowered, not just photographed. I have a deep connection with Latina brides. I understand the culture, the family dynamics, and the emotion of celebrating love in a way that honors where you come from. My goal is for you to feel confident, understood, and beautifully represented while making your family an essential part of the story too.

Based in New Jersey and New York, I travel wherever love takes me.
